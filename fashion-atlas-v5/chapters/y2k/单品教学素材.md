## 轨道静默 / Courrèges

### 立领几何短裙 · Geometric high-neck mini dress

参考归属：Courrèges

参考2024秋冬系列的包裹轮廓；重设衣长、领口和分割线。

[品牌系列出处](https://www.courreges.com/fr/page/collection-fall-winter-2024)

The fitted mini dress creates a narrow silhouette. Its high neckline and geometric panels give the bodice a structured appearance. Fitted describes how closely it follows the body; structured describes how clearly the shape is held.

贴体短裙构成窄长廓形，高领与几何拼片让衣身呈现明确结构。fitted 强调贴体程度，structured 强调保形的结构感。

观察：先看全身外轮廓，再看领口和拼片；不要把“贴身”与“有结构”当成同义词。

重点词：silhouette, fitted, geometric, structured, neckline, hem

随堂题：Which phrase describes the dress’s overall outline?

- A. a frayed hem

- B. a fitted silhouette

- C. a washed finish

答案：a fitted silhouette

silhouette 指整体廓形，fitted 描述贴体。

### 冰蓝透明肩罩 · Translucent blue capelet

参考归属：原创搭配

肩罩为原创外层，增加几何边缘与轻薄层次。

The translucent capelet lets light pass through, so the arms remain partly visible. Its light texture contrasts with the solid-looking boots. Here, contrast comes from different surface effects rather than strongly different colours.

半透明肩罩让光线穿过，手臂仍部分可见。轻薄质感与靴子较实的表面形成对比；这里的 contrast 来自表面效果，而非强烈色差。

观察：看肩罩与手臂重叠的位置，观察透光与不透光的差别。

重点词：translucent, texture, contrast

随堂题：The arm remains partly visible because the capelet is ___.

- A. translucent

- B. oversized

- C. washed

答案：translucent

透光并保留部分身体可见度，对应 translucent。

金属光泽长靴从光滑表面反光，长靴筒延伸了短裙下方的廓形。metallic 描述视觉表面，不等于整只靴子由实心金属制成。

The cropped denim jacket has oversized shoulders and a washed finish. Pale areas describe the surface treatment. A distressed effect can suggest wear, but washed denim does not necessarily have a frayed edge: frayed specifically means loose threads along an edge.

短丹宁夹克采用宽肩和洗水表面，浅色区域体现处理效果。distressed 可以表示做旧，但 washed 不一定带 frayed 毛边；frayed 特指边缘松散的线头。

随堂题：Which detail is clearly used to describe this jacket?

- A. a transparent knitted body

- B. oversized shoulders and a washed finish

答案：oversized shoulders and a washed finish

介绍明确指出宽肩和洗水；没有说它有透明针织衣身或长拖尾。

The wide-leg trousers carry more volume than the short jacket. An angled waistband and curved seams guide the eye across the hips and down the legs. The buckle is hardware: a small metal fastening, not a type of fabric.

### 紫色青柠边背心 · Fitted lilac vest with lime trim

参考归属：原创搭配

背心为原创内层，用轻薄色块平衡重丹宁。

The fitted vest sits close to the body under the oversized jacket. This layered outfit uses a small, bright inner piece to balance the heavier denim. The contrast is about fit, colour and texture at the same time.

贴体背心位于宽大夹克内侧。层叠造型用小而明亮的内层平衡较重的丹宁，同时在贴合度、颜色与肌理上形成对比。

观察：比较背心与夹克的宽窄，理解 layered 不只是“多穿一件”。

随堂题：Why does the fitted vest work with the oversized jacket?

- A. It creates a contrast in fit and volume.

- B. It hides every denim surface.

- C. It makes both pieces equally wide.

答案：It creates a contrast in fit and volume.

贴体内层与宽大外层形成体积和贴合度的对照。

### 银黑厚底鞋 · Silver-and-black platform shoes

参考归属：原创搭配

鞋履为原创方头厚底；播放器同为原创道具。

A thick platform sole gives the shoes visual weight below the wide trousers. The metallic toe area echoes the outfit’s hardware. The sole belongs to the shoe’s structure, while metallic describes a surface effect.

厚底在阔腿裤下方增加视觉重量，鞋头金属光泽呼应服装配件。sole 属于鞋的结构，metallic 则描述表面效果。

随堂题：“Platform sole” describes ___, while “metallic” describes ___.

- A. fabric colour / garment length

- B. shoe structure / surface effect

答案：shoe structure / surface effect

厚底是结构，金属光泽是表面效果。

## 液态回声 / Blumarine

### 不对称抽褶短裙 · Asymmetric draped mini dress

参考归属：Blumarine

参考2023春夏流动轮廓；紫色、单侧袖与短裙比例重新设计。

The lilac dress is draped around the body, and its asymmetric hem extends on one side. The fabric looks fluid because the folds continue into a moving tail. The short front and longer side create a deliberate proportion rather than a cutting mistake.

淡紫裙以垂褶围绕身体，不对称下摆在一侧延长。褶皱延伸成流动裙尾；前短侧长是有意的比例设计，并非裁剪失误。

答案：One side extends farther than the other.

asymmetric 指左右不对称，这里体现在下摆长度差。

One arm is covered by a sheer sleeve; the other uses an open knit layer with visible gaps. Both reveal part of the arm, but their textures are different. Sheer describes the transparent effect, while knit names a textile construction.

一侧手臂覆轻薄透视袖，另一侧是带可见空隙的镂空针织层。两者都露出部分手臂，肌理却不同；sheer 描述透视效果，knit 指针织结构。

透视是视觉属性，针织是织物结构，不能互相替代。

### 用边缘构造未来 / A Future Built from Edges

155 words · 本节原创读图课文；品牌原图供对照

#### P1

Look first at Orbital Silence, our original card inspired by a Courrèges reference. The white dress has a fitted silhouette, while its high neckline and angular panels give the body a structured outline. The shape does not depend on a large print. Instead, the edges do much of the visual work.

先看以 Courrèges 为参考设计的原创卡「轨道静默」。白裙廓形贴体，高领与棱角分割构成明确的结构。造型不靠大面积印花，而让边缘承担主要视觉作用。

#### P2

Now compare the shoulder cape with the boots. The cape is translucent: light passes through it, and the body remains partly visible. The metallic boots reflect light from a more solid surface. This contrast in texture makes the outfit interesting even though its colours stay close to white, blue and silver.

再比较肩罩与靴子：肩罩半透明，光线可以穿过，身体仍部分可见；金属光泽的靴子则从更实的表面反光。即使颜色集中在白、蓝、银，质感对比仍让造型有趣。

#### P3

Calling the look futuristic is a visual interpretation, not a claim that these clothes contain new technology. The card changes the reference into a short geometric dress and a separate cape. To describe it precisely, name a shape, a material effect and the relationship between them. “Shiny” alone tells us very little.

“未来感”是视觉判断，不代表衣服含有新科技。卡面将参考转译为几何短裙与独立肩罩。精确描述需要指出形状、材质效果及其关系，只有“闪亮”远远不够。

#### P3

卡面从 Diesel 参考出发，改变衣长、腰部结构与分割线。小型音乐设备提供故事线索，却不能证明整套衣服真实存在于 2000 年。先描述可见细节，再判断年代。

### 未来里的过去 / The Past Inside the Future

166 words · 本节原创比较阅读；公开文章为延伸阅读

#### P1

A fashion revival is not a time machine. People can borrow an older visual language while changing the cut, fabric and way an outfit is worn. Nostalgia describes the feeling attached to the past; revival describes a return to popularity. One is an emotion, while the other is a change in fashion.

时尚复兴不是时光机。人们借用过去的视觉语言，同时改变剪裁、面料与穿法。nostalgia 是对过去的情绪，revival 是重新流行；一个是感受，一个是时尚的变化。

#### P2

Our three cards make this distinction useful. Orbital Silence depends on a structured shape and a contrast between translucent and metallic surfaces. Distorted Frequency uses washed denim, curved seams and an oversized upper outline. Liquid Echo replaces that weight with fluid, draped fabric and an asymmetric hem. Their differences are part of the chapter, not mistakes to remove.

三张卡让这种区别有了具体对象：「轨道静默」依靠结构及半透明与金属表面的对比；「失真频段」使用洗水丹宁、弧形接缝与宽肩；「液态回声」改用流动垂褶与不对称下摆。差别本身就是本节内容。

#### P3

Imagine choosing a caption for the collection. “Three silver outfits from 2000” would be inaccurate: the designs are new and the colours differ. A stronger caption would explain how each outfit builds its own version of a remembered future. In fashion English, a convincing interpretation starts with visible evidence and keeps historical claims separate from invented scenes.

如果为整组写文案，“三套来自 2000 年的银色服装”并不准确：设计是新的，颜色也不同。更好的说明是，每套如何构建记忆中的未来。可靠的时尚表达从可见证据出发，将历史事实与创作场景分开。
